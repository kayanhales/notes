>How I Troubleshot Missing iPhone Texts After Switching to Android.

## What I switched

I moved from an iPhone 16 Pro on AT&T to a Google Pixel 11 on Mint Mobile. I took advantage of an early Black Friday deal Google was offering on the Pixel. I first moved my number to Mint and activated it on the iPhone, then transferred the service to the Pixel. I had switched carriers and phones in quick succession, and that made it hard to tell whether the missing messages were an Apple/iMessage issue, a Mint activation or routing issue, or a bit of both.

## The problem

After the switch, **texts from Android users were coming through, but I was not receiving messages from iPhone users**. Some people could see that I had texted them, but their replies never showed up on my Pixel. That was especially confusing because I had already turned off iMessage and FaceTime.

For two days I kept trying to work out where the messages were going. I talked with ChatGPT, Gemini, Mint Mobile support, and Apple Support. Apple's deregistration page gave me a service-unavailable error in my regular browser, and Apple Support closed our conversation before the issue was resolved. That left me frustrated and feeling like I was going in circles. Mint Mobile support was genuinely helpful. Eventually, after all the troubleshooting and some time for things to settle, messages from iPhone users started coming through again. I still can't say with certainty which individual step fixed it.

## The checklist I worked through

- **Step 1: Deregister your number from Apple in an incognito/private browser window.** Use Apple's official deregistration page to remove your number from iMessage after moving to a non-Apple phone. When I tried the page in my regular browser, I got a service error. I opened it in an incognito window, and the deregistration worked seamlessly. This tells Apple's systems that your number should no longer be associated with iMessage.
- **Step 2: Turn off iMessage on your other Apple devices.** Check any Macs, iPads, or other Apple devices still signed in to Messages. I turned off iMessage and signed out of Messages on my Apple devices so they would not keep receiving conversations tied to my Apple account.
- **Step 3: Turn off iMessage on the old iPhone and refresh its mobile network settings.** If you still have the iPhone, confirm iMessage is off there too. I reset the iPhone's mobile network settings as part of troubleshooting.
- **Step 4: Leave the old iPhone powered off for a while.** I put it in Airplane Mode and powered it down overnight. This was one troubleshooting step, but I can't prove that it caused the fix.
- **Step 5: Refresh the new Android phone's mobile network settings.** Check that the Mint eSIM and mobile service are active, then reset the Pixel's mobile network settings. I did this on the Android phone too. A network-settings reset can remove saved network settings, so check what your device will clear before proceeding.

## Pro tip: give it until the next business day

If you make the switch on a weekend, consider giving the number and messaging services until the next business day, Monday through Friday, before treating the problem as a failed transfer. That gives you time for any Apple account changes or carrier activation and provisioning updates to settle, and it means carrier support is available to check the line or escalate the issue if texts are still missing.

My guess is that the delay can help because two systems may be involved: Apple's records about whether your number is registered for iMessage, and Mint's activation and SMS routing for the number on its network. Those systems do not necessarily update at the same moment. But this is a practical guess, not a confirmed explanation of what fixed my messages. Carrier networks operate every day, so waiting for Monday is not a guarantee that routing only updates on weekdays; the main advantages are allowing time and having support teams available.

## What I learned

The switch itself was exciting—I was ready to try the Pixel—but not receiving replies from iPhone users made the first couple of days stressful and confusing. It felt like I had done the obvious things and still couldn't tell whether people were replying. I was relieved when the messages finally started arriving. I appreciate Mint Mobile support for helping me work through it, and I wish Apple Support had stayed with the issue until we had a resolution.

This is my experience, not a guaranteed fix for every iPhone-to-Android switch. If you are troubleshooting the same problem, keep track of which messages are missing, try iMessage deregistration in a private browser window, and ask your carrier to verify that your number and SMS service are fully active.
